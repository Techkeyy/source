## Evidence conventions

- FACT: directly supported by an official source or recorded probe.
- INFERENCE: reasoning from facts and product constraints.
- HYPOTHESIS: requires future user or product testing.
- PASS: observed success for the stated probe.
- HOLD: plausible but not yet proven or awaiting an owner decision.
- KILL: rejected for safety, scope, terms, or lack of product value.
